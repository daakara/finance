1. SUBSCRIBER INFRASTRUCTURE AND ONBOARDING
Subscribers must configure and maintain a Dissemination Receipt Server (DRS) with a static public IP address, authorized SSH key credentials, and automated ingestion software capable of receiving push feeds from the PDS primary and failover dissemination hosts.

2. TRANSMISSION, RETRANSMISSION, AND FAILOVER RECOVERY
The PDS system implements multi-tiered delivery assurance:
  2.1. Automated Retransmission: PDS automatically attempts transmission and retransmission of each submission upon temporary DRS unavailability or transport failure.
  2.2. Staging Area Redirection: If a subscriber DRS experiences persistent receipt failures, the PDS Helpdesk may redirect the subscriber's feed to an isolated staging area to prevent pipeline backpressure on other subscribers.
  2.3. PDS FTP Historical Recovery: A dedicated PDS FTP server maintains a rolling five-day (5-day) historical archive of all disseminated files (*.dissem, *.paper, *.pr, *.pc) and daily audit logs (dissem*.EOD). Subscribers may retrieve missed or corrupt files via FTP recovery.
  2.4. Helpdesk Support: The PDS Operations Contractor provides manual retransmission support upon receipt of verified gaps identified via the daily EOD audit file.

3. CROSS-DAY RETRANSMISSIONS AND RECORD KEEPING
Files recovered or retransmitted on Day D+1 that originated on Business Day D retain their original source-day filename, internal headers, and acceptance timestamps. Subscribers must distinguish between local receipt timestamp and source business day attribution.

4. ADMINISTRATIVE AND CONTRACTUAL GOVERNANCE
Subscription agreements are administered in coordination with the Commission's designated PDS operations contractor. Service is subject to monthly subscription fees and annual renewal terms.

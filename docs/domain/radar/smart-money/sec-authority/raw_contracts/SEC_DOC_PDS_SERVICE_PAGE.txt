1. SERVICE DESCRIPTION
The EDGAR Public Dissemination Service (PDS) provides a dedicated, real-time feed of all public filings accepted by the SEC's EDGAR system. It is designed for commercial information vendors, researchers, and institutional subscribers who require automated, high-volume receipt of filings as they are processed.

2. RELATIONSHIP TO FREE PUBLIC ACCESS
The SEC provides free public access to all EDGAR filings via SEC.gov, EDGAR Company Search, RSS feeds, and public daily/quarterly index files. PDS is a fee-based transmission service and does not convey proprietary rights, exclusive access, or non-public disclosure advantages.

3. DATA FORMAT AND CHARACTERISTICS
Disseminated records include complete submission envelopes with original SGML/XML headers, primary documents, and exhibits. Electronic submissions are delivered in standard ASCII formats.

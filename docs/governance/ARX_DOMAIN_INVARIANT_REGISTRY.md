### EPISTEMIC & COMPUTATIONAL TRUTH INVARIANTS

#### `ARX_INV_001`: UNKNOWN_NEVER_BECOMES_AUTHENTIC_ZERO
- **Contract:** An unobserved, missing, failed, or out-of-bounds metric must be represented explicitly as `None`, `null`, `UNAVAILABLE`, or `ERROR`.
- **Violation:** Substituting numerical `0.0` or `0` for missing metrics (e.g. 0 drawdown, 0 debt, 0 short interest, 0 PE ratio, 0 cash runway).

#### `ARX_INV_002`: SYNTHETIC_EVIDENCE_NEVER_BECOMES_NATURAL_EVIDENCE
- **Contract:** Any data generated by fixtures, replay harnesses, mock generators, or static placeholder defaults must be tagged with synthetic provenance. Under no circumstances may synthetic data enter production scoring, live ledgers, empirical evaluation denominators, or user-facing live market dashboards.
- **Violation:** Hardcoded live market prices (e.g., SPY 542.10, VIX 15.20) or hardcoded radar divergence datasets rendered with live institutional badges.

#### `ARX_INV_003`: UNKNOWN_NEVER_BECOMES_FAVORABLE
- **Contract:** In the presence of missing information, risk, safety, and qualification algorithms must fail closed. Missing data must never produce a more favorable risk assessment or higher conviction score than complete data.
- **Violation:** Exception during drawdown analysis returning `0.0` drawdown or `Moderate` risk level with score `50`.

#### `ARX_INV_004`: TEST_EXECUTION_NEVER_MUTATES_PRODUCTION_EVIDENCE
- **Contract:** The execution of test suites, test runners, or validation scripts must result in a physical byte delta of zero (`TEST_EXECUTION_PRODUCTION_BYTE_DELTA = 0`) across all production-owned ledgers and SQLite databases.
- **Violation:** Automated tests appending dummy trades to `paper_trading_ledger.json` or inserting test rows into `governance.db`.

#### `ARX_INV_005`: ETF_IDENTITY_REQUIRES_SERIES_LEVEL_ISOLATION
- **Contract:** In multi-series SEC registrants, series identity is defined uniquely by EDGAR `Series ID` (`S000...`) and `Class Contract ID` (`C000...`). Positive issuer, CIK, or filename similarity must never override negative or contradictory Series ID evidence.
- **Violation:** Mapping a target Series to a sibling Series within the same CIK based solely on shared words or registrant accession.

#### `ARX_INV_006`: WEAK_IDENTITY_EVIDENCE_NEVER_OVERRIDES_EXACT_IDENTITY_CONTRADICTION
- **Contract:** Exact contradictory identity tokens (different Series ID, different Class ID, distinct legal name) take absolute precedence over token overlap or partial text matches.
- **Violation:** Accepting a single-fund prospectus containing an explicit conflicting Series ID because the fund name has 2 overlapping words.

#### `ARX_INV_007`: PROVIDER_FAILURE_NEVER_BECOMES_AUTHENTIC_ZERO
- **Contract:** An outage, HTTP 5xx error, or timeout from an external data provider (Alpaca, EODHD, FRED, SEC) must be propagated as `UNAVAILABLE` or `PROVIDER_ERROR`. It must never collapse to 0 events, 0 trades, empty holdings, or neutral conviction.
- **Violation:** Market command ribbon showing static 2026 data as live feed when FastAPI or external market feed is unreachable.

#### `ARX_INV_008`: STALE_DATA_NEVER_PRESENTED_AS_LIVE
- **Contract:** Any observation whose timestamp falls outside the current market session freshness window must be visibly labeled as `STALE`, `CACHED`, or `SETTLEMENT_PINNED`.
- **Violation:** Displaying hours-old intraday candles or static fallback snapshots without freshness disclaimers.

#### `ARX_INV_009`: PROSPECTIVE_EVIDENCE_NEVER_USES_POST_BOUNDARY_INFORMATION
- **Contract:** In historical research, walk-forward testing, and empirical evaluation, no algorithm may access observations, filings, price changes, or metadata dated after the decision timestamp.
- **Violation:** Using post-boundary SEC filings or retrospective restatements to classify historical fund mandates.

#### `ARX_INV_010`: REPORT_TOTALS_MUST_RECONCILE_FROM_TARGET_LEVEL_STATES
- **Contract:** Summary reports and dashboard KPIs must be derived as strict arithmetic sums from immutable target-level ledgers. Reports must never assemble denominators from ad-hoc counters or narrative assumptions.
- **Violation:** Folding cache-miss targets into absent counts or double-counting ambiguous targets.

#### `ARX_INV_011`: INDICATOR_SEMANTICS_REQUIRE_STRICT_BURN_IN
- **Contract:** A technical indicator with an N-period window (e.g. SMA50, RSI14, ATR14) requires at least N valid closed bars of historical observations. Partial-window computations must never masquerade as the full-window indicator, nor may neutral constants (e.g., RSI=50.0) be manufactured when history is insufficient.
- **Violation:** Calculating 14-period RSI or ATR with `min_periods=1` or substituting 50.0 when bars are missing.

#### `ARX_INV_012`: DERIVED_CACHE_KEYS_REQUIRE_FULL_SEMANTIC_VERSIONING
- **Contract:** Any cache key for derived financial or research outputs must incorporate the source identity, source content hash, engine code version, and policy version.
- **Violation:** Checkpoint store or cache keyed solely on `symbol` without parser or resolver version tags.

#### `ARX_INV_013`: STATISTICAL_CONFIDENCE_REQUIRES_EMPIRICAL_PROOF
- **Contract:** Terms such as "95% Statistical Confidence", "Statistical Significance", or "Confidence Interval" may only be emitted when supported by formal parametric or non-parametric statistical tests (t-stat, p-value, standard error, sample size $N \ge 30$).
- **Violation:** Asserting "95% Statistical Confidence" on an uncalibrated heuristic accuracy score.

## 3. Epics Architecture (AI-100 through AI-500)

### Epic AI-100: Adaptive User Modeling
- **AI-101 (Decision DNA Engine):** Dynamic profile quantifying risk appetite, bias propensities (loss aversion, disposition effect), and conviction stability.
- **AI-102 (Behavior Pattern Detection):** Real-time detection of overconfidence, late momentum chases, premature profit-taking, and rule drift.
- **AI-103 (Adaptive Learning Profile):** Identifies the optimal pedagogical format for each operator (visual simulation, numerical proof, counterfactual stress testing).

### Epic AI-200: Adaptive Coaching Engine
- **AI-201 (Contextual Coaching):** Replaces static advice with situation-specific micro-nudges at the exact moment of order staging.
- **AI-202 (Weakness-Specific Coaching):** Detects regime-specific vulnerabilities (e.g. macro stagflation drift) and automatically serves targeted playbooks.
- **AI-203 (Progressive Coaching Matrix):** Evolves across four capability tiers:
  $$\text{Beginner (Education)} \longrightarrow \text{Intermediate (Execution)} \longrightarrow \text{Advanced (Optimization)} \longrightarrow \text{Optimizer (Micro-calibration)}$$

### Epic AI-300: Adaptive Workspaces
- **AI-301 (Adaptive Executive Home):** Dynamically reconfigures hierarchy based on operator role, recent decision stress, and market volatility.
- **AI-302 (Adaptive Morning Briefing):** Surfaces only the highest-impact opportunities and tail risks relevant to the specific user's portfolio.
- **AI-303 (Dynamic Workspace Simplification):** Progressively hides low-utility telemetry and expands high-conviction decision surfaces.

### Epic AI-400: Adaptive Capability Optimization
- **AI-401 (Capability Selection Engine):** Algorithmic router matching user personas to capabilities with the highest historical CIE.
- **AI-402 (Capability Recommendation Engine):** Automated discovery mechanism surfacing high-impact, low-adoption "hidden gems" (high CAE).
- **AI-403 (Capability Portfolio Adaptation):** Continuously reallocates institutional capability budgets across Portfolios A, B, C, and D.

### Epic AI-500: Autonomous Intelligence Governance
- **AI-501 (Adaptive Safety Layer):** Enforces human-in-the-loop sign-offs for all capital allocation decisions (zero unapproved autonomous execution).
- **AI-502 (Adaptive Explainability):** Every adaptive recommendation exposes 4-part rationale: *Why this? Why now? Expected impact? Statistical confidence?*
- **AI-503 (Adaptive Intervention Audit):** Immutable ledger mapping:
  $$\text{Intervention} \longrightarrow \text{Behavioral Delta} \longrightarrow \text{Outcome Hit Rate} \longrightarrow \text{Realized Value}$$

## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Mission

Phase 28 Milestone 2B establishes the **My Evolution Workspace** (`MY EVOLUTION`), delivering the core transformation from a diagnostic metrics engine into a **Personal Decision Development Platform**. 

While the Executive Narrative Home (Milestone 2A) tells executives *what changed today*, My Evolution Workspace proves *what kind of decision-maker they are becoming* and provides the roadmap to reach **Institutional Optimizer** status (DIR 80).

### Core Executive Questions Answered:
1. *What kind of decision maker am I becoming?*
2. *Which habits drove my score improvement?*
3. *Which habits are holding me back?*
4. *What is my exact path to the top 5% (Score 80)?*

### Scope Governance & Behavioral Boundary
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Zero Financial Trading Recommendations**:  
> In strict compliance with the Phase 26 Quantitative Freeze and model governance protocols, all guidance generated by the Evolution Engine strictly governs **personal decision-making habits and discipline** (e.g. stop-loss adherence, outcome reviews, pre-mortem friction, position sizing limits). Zero financial asset selection or automated trade allocation recommendations are produced.

## 2. Core Architectural Governance Invariants

### 2.1 Invariant INV-B9: Improvement Traceability Invariant
- **Definition**: 100% of Decision Improvement Rating (DIR) movement across quarterly milestones must resolve through an audited causal chain:
  $$\text{DIR Delta} \longrightarrow \text{Capability} \longrightarrow \text{Behavior Adopted/Stopped} \longrightarrow \text{Outcome Impact}$$
- **Attribution Coverage Floor**: Explained capability improvements must account for $\ge 95.0\%$ of historical DIR movement:
  $$\text{Attribution Coverage} = \frac{\sum \Delta\text{DIR}_{\text{explained}}}{\Delta\text{DIR}_{\text{total}}} \times 100 \ge 95.0\%$$
- **Drift Stability**: Unexplained residual drift must remain strictly $< 1.0$ DQ points across historical quarters (audited at $0.6$ pts).
- **Monotonic Habit Gains**: Behavioral adoption correlates positively with DIR movement, and repeat mistake rates decrease monotonically ($42\% \to 11\%$).

### 2.2 Invariant INV-B10: Behavior Conservation & Cohort Invariant
- **Definition**: Behavioral gains cannot be double-counted across overlapping platform capabilities. Every single point of DIR improvement must map to exactly one primary driver.
- **Conservation Formula**:
  $$\text{Discrepancy} = \left| \sum_{k} \Delta\text{DIR}_k - \Delta\text{DIR}_{\text{total, explained}} \right| = 0.0$$
- **Single Cohort Assignment**: Users are deterministically assigned to exactly one primary behavioral cohort (`Autonomous Learner` / `Advanced Improvers`).
- **Uncertainty Bounds**: Forward projections include rigorous Wilson-score $95\%$ confidence bounds ($[78, 83]$ for Target 80).

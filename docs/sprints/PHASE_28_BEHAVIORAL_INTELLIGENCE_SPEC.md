# ARX Terminal vNext: Phase 28 Behavioral Intelligence Specification
## Story-First Decision Operating System & Behavioral Improvement Framework

**Strategic Mission**: Transition from *Decision Intelligence* into **Decision Improvement Intelligence**.  
**Core Paradigm**: Move from a metrics-first analytics platform to a **story-first decision operating system** that continuously develops and improves decision-makers.  
**Audience**: Executive Leadership, CIO Desk, Portfolio Managers, Quantitative Analysts, Governance Board.  

---

## 1. The Core Philosophy Shift

```
Traditional Analytics Platform:
"What happened?"  (Metrics, Charts, KPIs, P&L)

Phase 28 Decision Improvement Operating System:
"Why it matters"  →  "What changed"  →  "What to do next"  →  "What outcomes are likely"
```

Within 5 seconds of opening the terminal, an executive knows:
1. **Am I improving?** &rarr; *Decision Quality 74 (▲ +12 pts, Top 18% cohort)*
2. **What helped?** &rarr; *Institutional Flow Discipline (+6.2 quality pts, 91% confidence)*
3. **What hurts?** &rarr; *Macro Deterioration Blindness (21% risk exposure, 87% confidence)*
4. **What should I do today?** &rarr; *Reduce exposure in weakening regimes (+3.4 pts impact, 89% confidence)*

## 2. Epic Architecture & Scope

### EPIC BI-100: Story-First Executive Home
- **Glanceable Executive Header**: Personalized greeting (`GOOD MORNING DAVID — Monday, September 7, 2026`).
- **5-Second Matrix**: Four primary cards (Decision Quality 74, Biggest Positive Change, Biggest Risk Exposure, Recommended Focus Today).
- **Today's Story**: Weekly narrative (Followed 84% recommendations, DQS +2 points, Repeat mistakes -12%, Macro exposure alert).
- **Outcome Narrative**: Monthly recap (42 decisions: 30 won, 12 failed; Net learning: *Position timing remains highest leverage improvement area*).
- **AI Chief of Staff Directive**: *"If you only do one thing today: Reduce exposure in weakening macro regimes. Estimated drawdown reduction: -4.2%, Confidence: 92%."*
- **Responsive Standard**: Desktop 12-col / Mobile 390px (iPhone 15 Pro) single-column with zero horizontal overflow.
- **Telemetry Contracts**: `executive_home_viewed`, `story_module_viewed`.

### EPIC BI-200: Morning Briefing 2.0 (Story Flow)
- **4-Stage Narrative Sequence**:
  $$\mathbf{Market\ Changed} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Why\ It\ Matters} \longrightarrow \mathbf{What\ Is\ Affected} \longrightarrow \mathbf{What\ To\ Do}$$
- **Overnight Risk Shift**: Market Risk Score 42 $\to$ 56.
- **Market Shifts**:
  - *Treasuries (10Y Yield)*: Strengthened (Flight to quality, -8 bps).
  - *Semiconductors (SOX)*: Weakened (Break of 20-day moving average).
  - *Growth Breadth*: Narrowed (32% Russell 1000 above 50-day SMA).
- **Portfolio Exposure**: 3 positions violating preferred conditions (NVDA, AMD, CRWD), $\$184,000$ capital at risk, $91\%$ confidence.
- **Fail-Safe Invariant**: If market feed is disrupted, renders explicit staleness banner and suppresses low-confidence guidance.
- **Telemetry Contracts**: `briefing_narrative_viewed`, `impacted_positions_viewed`.

### EPIC BI-300: Behavioral Intelligence Center & Behavioral Timeline

  4. *Repeat Mistakes*: 21 $\to$ 12 (-43% reduction)
  5. *Decision Drift*: 21.0% (LOW RISK)

  - *Q2 2026*: Macro regime blindness &rarr; Macro Gating filter &rarr; +3.0 quality points.

- **Telemetry Contracts**: `behavioral_center_viewed`, `behavior_change_viewed`.

### EPIC BI-400: Learning Velocity Engine
- **Learning Velocity Index (LVI)**:
  $$\mathbf{LVI} = (\text{Decision Quality Growth} \times 0.5) + (\text{Behavioral Adoption} \times 0.3) + (\text{Rule Adherence} \times 0.2)$$
  - Raw Score: $(12 \times 0.5) + (70.5 \times 0.3) + (87 \times 0.2) = 44.55$
  - Normalized Score: **`84 / 100`** (*HIGH, Top 12% peer decile*).
- **Behavioral Maturity Index (BMI)**:
  $$\mathbf{BMI} = 0.25(\text{DQS}) + 0.25(\text{BAR}) + 0.20(\text{Rule Adherence}) + 0.15(\text{LVI}) + 0.15(\text{Drift Control}) = \mathbf{78.0 / 100}$$
- **Improvement Momentum**: $\frac{+6}{+4} = \mathbf{1.5}$ ($>1.0$ Accelerating).
- **Telemetry Contract**: `lvi_viewed`.

### EPIC BI-500: AI Behavioral Coach & Forward Forecast
- **Forward Trajectory Guidance**:
  *"You're improving because: 1. Better position sizing, 2. Stronger macro discipline, 3. Fewer momentum chases. Continue this path and you'll likely reach a quality score of 80 within 4 months. Confidence: 87%."*
- **Scenario Simulation**: "Accelerate Drift &le; 15%" &rarr; shortens trajectory to 2.5 months.
- **Uncertainty Downgrade Guard**: If confidence $< 60\%$, system triggers an uncertainty warning and visually downgrades forecast confidence.
- **Telemetry Contract**: `behavioral_forecast_viewed`.

### EPIC BI-600: Behavioral Cohorts & Statistical Confidence Bands
- **Wilson Score 95% Confidence Intervals**:
  $$\mathbf{SE} = \sqrt{\frac{p(1-p)}{n}}, \quad 95\%\text{ CI} = p \pm 1.96 \times \mathbf{SE}$$
  - Mentor Engagement: **67.4%** [95% CI: 64.2% - 70.6%]
  - Behavioral Adoption (BAR): **70.5%** [95% CI: 68.1% - 72.7%]
  - Rule Adherence: **87.0%** [95% CI: 84.8% - 89.0%]
- **Visual Confidence Band Rendering**: `67.4% [━━━━━━●━━━━━━] 64.2% - 70.6%`.
- **5-Tier Trend Velocity**:
  - $\ge +10\%$: `RAPID_IMPROVEMENT`
  - $+3\%$ to $+10\%$: `IMPROVING`
  - $-3\%$ to $+3\%$: `STABLE`
  - $-10\%$ to $-3\%$: `DECLINING`
  - $< -10\%$: `CRITICAL_DECLINE`
- **5 Behavioral Maturity Tiers**: Consumers (18%), Investigators (22%), Practitioners (29%), Learners (20%), Optimizers (11%).
- **Role-Based Adoption Benchmarking**: Portfolio Managers (82%), Executives (76%), Analysts (61%), New Users (49%).
- **Telemetry Contract**: `cohort_comparison_viewed`.

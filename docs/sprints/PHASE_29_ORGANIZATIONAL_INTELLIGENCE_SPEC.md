## 2. The Organizational Decision Effectiveness Index (ODEI)

### 2.1 Formula Definition (Locked v1.0)
$$\text{ODEI} = 0.35 \times \text{DQ} + 0.30 \times \text{OE} + 0.20 \times \text{LE} + 0.15 \times \text{OH}$$

Where:
- **$\text{DQ}$ (Decision Quality, 35%):** Average institutional decision quality across committees, teams, and senior analysts.
- **$\text{OE}$ (Outcome Effectiveness, 30%):** Realized outcome hit rates, capital preservation ratio, and risk-adjusted excess returns.
- **$\text{LE}$ (Learning Effectiveness, 20%):** Knowledge reuse rate, best practice propagation velocity, and repeat mistake reduction.
- **$\text{OH}$ (Organizational Health, 15%):** Rule adherence stability, dissent variance, and behavioral drift minimization.

## 4. Governance Invariants (INV-OI1 through INV-OI11)

1. **INV-OI1 (Organizational Traceability):** Every institutional outcome must trace backwards: $\text{Outcome} \to \text{Decision} \to \text{Committee} \to \text{Evidence}$.
2. **INV-OI2 (Collective Attribution Completeness):** Attribution of organizational performance across Individual, Team, Committee, and System dimensions must sum to $100.0\%$.
3. **INV-OI3 (Organizational Consistency):** Identical inputs must deterministically produce identical ODEI scores and narratives across all runs.
4. **INV-OI4 (Influence Transparency):** All contributing actors and decision weights must be exposed with $100\%$ visibility.
5. **INV-OI5 (Groupthink Detection):** Unanimous consensus without dissent or evidence diversity automatically raises a formal Groupthink Risk flag.
6. **INV-OI6 (Benchmark Isolation):** No team or committee benchmark may include the subject team within its comparative baseline.
7. **INV-OI7 (Learning Conservation):** Total learning improvement delta must equal Attributed Gains plus Residual with $\le 1.0\%$ tolerance.
8. **INV-OI8 (Organizational Fairness):** No single actor or team may account for $>40.0\%$ of total institutional influence without triggering a Concentration Risk warning.
9. **INV-OI9 (Recommendation Explainability):** Every organizational action proposal must expose Evidence, Learning, Benchmark, Impact, and Confidence.
10. **INV-OI10 (Institutional Memory Integrity):** Historical decision, prediction, and outcome records are immutable and tamper-proof.
11. **INV-OI11 (Institutional Learning Non-Regression):** Institutionalized best practices (Confidence $\ge 95\%$, $N \ge N_{min}$, Value $> 0$, Governance Approved) must never silently regress: $A(t) \ge B - 10\%$ and $E(t) \ge E_{\text{hist}} - 5\%$.

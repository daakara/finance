## Design Context

### Users
- **Primary Users**: Quantitative and disciplined retail traders, active day scalpers, and long-term fundamental compounders seeking Wall Street institutional-grade decision intelligence without subscription walls, speculative noise, or promotional hype.
- **Context of Use**: Fast live-market trading hours (9:30 AM – 4:00 PM EST) where milliseconds, clean legibility, and zero visual clutter are paramount, as well as evening / weekend fundamental research, portfolio stress-testing, and congressional STOCK Act filing audits.
- **Jobs to be Done**:
  1. Instantly validate trade risk/reward with mathematical Mark Minervini Stage 2 invalidation ladders and Cornish-Fisher VaR bounds.
  2. Filter high-probability momentum gems using Peter Lynch GARP and Joel Greenblatt Magic Formula screeners.
  3. Track US Congressional disclosures and insider accumulation vs. distribution traps with statutory latency metrics.
  4. Manage an encrypted, client-side zero-login paper portfolio.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice & Tone**: Precise, mathematical, institutional, honest, unhyped.
- **3-Word Personality**: Precise, High-Velocity, Uncompromising
- **Emotional Goals**: Confident decision-making, deep trust through transparent data provenance, calm and focused execution during high-volatility market events.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone**: Cyber-dark quantitative terminal with high-contrast information density, paired with an editorial Paper Light mode for deep research reading.
- **Color Philosophy**: Strict functional signal colors (Emerald for profit/accumulation, Rose for hard stops/drawdown, Cyan for primary interactive telemetry, Amber for volatility warnings, Purple for dark pool sweeps). Zero decorative rainbow noise.
- **Typography**: Monospace JetBrains Mono / SF Mono with tabular-nums for all prices, tickers, and risk metrics; crisp system sans-serif for editorial commentary and statutory disclosure notes.
- **Anti-References**: Generic crypto pump-and-dump sites, neon purple AI slop gradients, cluttered bloated legacy broker UIs, fake static price charts.

### Design Principles
1. **Fact-First, Mathematical Monotonicity**: Every metric must be mathematically derived and grounded in authentic upstream feeds; every trade idea must state its exact invalidation price.
2. **Zero False Movement & Instant Feedback**: Visual states and charts update optimistically (<10ms); responsive drawers and modals auto-fold to keep actionable telemetry above the fold.
3. **Dual-Horizon & Dual-Vernacular Ergonomics**: Provide 1-click lens switches between Day Trader (intraday ATR/RVOL) and Long-Term Investor (ROIC/Piotroski), and between Plain English and Pro Quant terminology.
4. **Privacy-First Zero-Login Architecture**: User data stays in local browser storage; analytics remain 100% cookieless and GDPR-compliant.

# Design Context: ARX Quantitative Terminal

## Design Context

### Users
- **Target Personas**: Quantitative traders, institutional analysts, retail momentum traders, and STOCK Act disclosure trackers.
- **The Day Trader (DAY_TRADER)**: Requires rapid situational awareness, 5-minute VWAP / 20 EMA candlestick alignment, 14-day ATR dollar range, bid-ask spread liquidity, and tactile position-sizing calculations with zero cognitive lag during active market sessions.
- **The Fundamental & Quantitative Investor (LONG_TERM)**: Requires institutional risk modeling (Cornish-Fisher VaR, Sortino, Omega ratio), Piotroski F-score health, Joel Greenblatt Magic Formula & Peter Lynch GARP screening, clinical pipeline catalysts, and multi-year DCF earnings growth trajectories.
- **Context of Use**: Desktop multi-monitor setups during fast trading sessions, and mobile phones for quick intraday alerts, setup tracking, and disclosure monitoring.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice & Tone**: Dense, opinionated, surgically precise, utilitarian, and uncompromisingly transparent.
- **3-Word Personality**: DENSE * OPINIONATED * SURGICALLY PRECISE
- **Emotional Goals**: High operational confidence, zero cognitive friction, technical rigor, institutional authority, and absolute data integrity.
- **Anti-References**: Banned generic AI slop aesthetics (floating cards with colorful gradient borders, glowing neon-cyan on pure pitch black, random uncalibrated sparklines, side-stripe card borders border-left: 3px solid, gradient text fills, hero metric cards with meaningless percentage pills, rounded SaaS templates).

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone**: Industrial Precision Mainframe / High-Density Quantitative Workstation (inspired by Bloomberg Terminal meets modern aerospace telemetry).
- **Theme**: Dark Engineered Canvas tinted toward deep Slate-Navy (oklch(0.12 0.015 250) / #070a11), high-contrast container surfaces (oklch(0.16 0.02 250)), and recessed telemetry wells (oklch(0.10 0.015 250)). Never pure #000000 or pure #ffffff.
- **Accents**:
  - Tactical Amber Gold (oklch(0.76 0.16 75) / #f59e0b) for Day Trader momentum, order execution, volatility alerts, and high-urgency catalysts.
  - Tactical Cyan (oklch(0.78 0.14 200) / #22d3ee) for Long-Term balance sheets, valuation models, and mathematical invariants.
  - Emerald Compounding (oklch(0.75 0.17 150)) & Rose Downside Risk (oklch(0.68 0.20 25)).
- **Typography**: Clean sans paired with dense tabular monospace (tabular-nums font-mono) for all financial metrics, prices, timestamps, coordinates, and percentages. Strict 65-75ch maximum line lengths on prose.

### Design Principles
1. **Concentric Border Radii**: Mathematical nesting: Outer Card rounded-2xl (16px) -> Inner Well rounded-xl (12px) -> Interactive Button/Badge rounded-lg (8px). Never repeat identical radii across nested containers.
2. **Tactile Micro-Interactions**: Active press scales at active:scale-[0.96] with rapid 100ms ease-out transitions for buttons, tabs, and filters.
3. **Tabular Numerals & Zero Jitter**: Every live financial data point, multiple, timestamp, or percentage must use tabular-nums font-mono to prevent layout shifts during WebSocket streams.
4. **Zero AI Slop**: Absolute bans on side-stripe accent borders, gradient text, decorative glassmorphism blur without purpose, fake metrics, and empty modals.
5. **Ergonomic Density**: Maintain high visual information density without clutter by using clear visual hierarchy, recessed wells, progressive disclosure, and minimum 40x40px touch targets on mobile.

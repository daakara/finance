# ARX Terminal — Frontend Design Context & Guidelines

## Design Context

### Users
- **Target Audience**: Discretionary swing traders, disciplined growth investors, quant enthusiasts, retail compounders, and financial analysts seeking hedge-fund grade market intelligence without the $24,000/yr Bloomberg Terminal barrier.
- **Context of Use**: Rapid pre-market / after-hours equity screening, active trade execution planning, Congressional STOCK Act anomaly monitoring, and risk management validation under live market conditions.
- **Primary Job to be Done**: Demystify complex institutional data (SEC Form 4 sweeps, Congressional committee jurisdiction overlaps, Minervini VCP stages, Cornish-Fisher VaR bounds) into risk-defined, actionable trade execution plans with zero emotional guesswork.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice & Tone**: Authoritative, mathematically rigorous, transparent, anti-hype, and educational.
- **3-Word Personality**: **Precise, Authoritative, Disciplined.**
- **Emotional Goals**: **Confident & In-Control.** Eradicates FOMO, retail gambling impulses, and panic selling by enforcing mathematical risk-reward corridors (Stop Loss < Entry < TP1 < TP2) and 5-point pre-flight clearance gates.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone**: **High-Density Cyber-Financial Terminal with Editorial Elegance.** Combines the command-center density of Palantir/Bloomberg with the polished typography and micro-interactions of modern craft software.
- **Color Architecture**:
  - *Obsidian Dark (Primary)*: Deep navy-black foundations (`#070a10`, `#111722`, `#162030`) with razor-sharp slate borders (`#243044`, `#364866`).
  - *Paper Light (Editorial Mode)*: Crisp financial press palette (`#f4f6f9`, `#ffffff`) with refined slate borders (`#e2e8f0`, `#cbd5e1`).
  - *Data Accents*: High-contrast neon semantics — **Cyber Cyan** (`#06b6d4` / `#0284c7`) for optimal accumulation zones and institutional signals, **Emerald** (`#10b981` / `#16a34a`) for take-profit targets and cleared gates, **Rose** (`#f43f5e` / `#dc2626`) for hard invalidation stops and hype traps, and **Amber** (`#f59e0b` / `#d97706`) for stage-4 caution and macro alerts.
- **Typography**: Clean system UI sans-serif for reading hierarchy paired with strict **monospace tabular figures** (`font-mono`, `tabular-nums`) for all prices, percentages, ratios, and econometric indicators to guarantee flawless columnar alignment.
- **References**: Palantir Gotham / Foundry, Bloomberg Terminal, TradingView Pro, Linear, Apple Developer Documentation.
- **Anti-References**: Generic "AI crypto casino" dashboards with fuzzy pastel gradients, floating useless cards, slow bloated parallax, meaningless marketing buzzwords, and vague hype indicators.

### Design Principles

1. **Dual-Vernacular Invariant (Plain English ⚡ vs. Pro Quant 🏛️)**
   - Every complex financial metric must be legible to both a beginner retail compounder and a veteran hedge fund analyst. Support instant, zero-reload switching between Plain English explanations (e.g. *"Safe Buying Price"*, *"Why to Exit"*) and Pro Quant semantics (*"Optimal Accumulation Corridor"*, *"ATR-Derived Invalidation Stop"*).

2. **High-Density Monospace Tabular Precision**
   - Financial data requires scanning efficiency. Use compact padding (`p-3.5`, `gap-2.5`), clear visual hierarchy, and `tabular-nums` on all currency, percentage, and mathematical values so columns never shift during price updates.

3. **Strict Risk-First Hierarchy (Capital Preservation Over Hype)**
   - Always display risk invalidation floors (Stop Loss, VaR risk, Distribution Traps) alongside or before upside profit targets. Make danger signals unmistakable without resorting to garish alarms.

4. **Tactile Micro-Interactions & Sub-Millisecond Feedback**
   - Interactive surfaces must feel responsive and physical: `active:scale-[0.98]`, subtle border glow on hover (`hover:border-cyan-500/80`), smooth badge pill transitions, and optimistic local state updates with `<10ms` perceived latency.

5. **Anti-Silo Component Ergonomics (One-Click Discovery-to-Action)**
   - Discovery cards must never be dead-ends. Every radar item, screener result, and congressional trade must link seamlessly into the Chart Terminal, the Pre-Flight Checklist, or the Paper Portfolio in 1 click.
